Operators in JabirScript

Operators are used to perform mathematical calculations and evaluate comparisons between data values within your scripts.

Assignment & Comparison Operators

Use itEquals for declaring variable values, and standard signs like ==, >, or < inside condition blocks to compare data.

thisIs score itEquals &50&??

checkIf score > &25& thenDo
    popout: &Passed!&??
closeCheck??

Arithmetic Operators

Perform math calculations using standard operators such as addition (+), subtraction (-), multiplication (*), and division (/).

thisIs total itEquals &10& + &5&??
popout: total??

Operator Tip: Always make sure comparison operators match the data type you are evaluating against your stored string or numeric variables.
