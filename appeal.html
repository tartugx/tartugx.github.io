Frequently Appealed Questions

How long does a decision take?

The Council meets every lunar cycle. Depending on Mars-to-Earth alignment, responses typically arrive within 3 to 5 Martian business days.

Can I pay to get unbanned?

While we do not accept direct bribes, upgrading to the $670/yr "Good boy" tier is viewed as an extraordinary act of contrition and automatically gets your record expunged.

What if Lionel Pessi banned me himself?

If the Head of Penalty Labs issued your ban, your chances are thin. You must submit a written apology directly addressed to Penalty Labs along with your appeal.
